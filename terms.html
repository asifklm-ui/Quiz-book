What The Quiz Book is

The Quiz Book is a free website of practice quizzes for kindergarten through grade 12, covering math, reading, science and social studies. You choose a subject and a grade, the quiz adjusts as you go, and you can save questions to a review pocket for later.

It is run as a free educational site. There are no adverts, nothing to buy, and no account that belongs to us. The whole site is a set of static pages served from a web host.

Questions, answers and accuracy

The questions are written and edited for this site. Even so, a bank of thousands of questions will contain the occasional error, an answer key that could be read two ways, or a question whose difficulty sits a grade higher or lower than the label suggests.

  • Always treat the site as practice. It is not an assessment, a grade, or a substitute for instruction.
  • No curriculum guarantee. The grades and topics are our own labels. They are not aligned to, endorsed by, or a substitute for any standard, exam board or syllabus.
  • If something looks wrong, mention it to a grown-up or teacher rather than accepting it. We would rather you trust your maths than our answer key.
  • Progress is encouragement, not evidence. The bars and streaks show how much you have practised, not how well you will do on a test.

Your practice and your privacy

Your practice is saved in your own browser. If you choose to turn on syncing, it is saved in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. We do not receive a copy of either. The details — including what is stored, how syncing works, and how to delete it — are in the privacy policy, which is part of these terms.

Because practice lives on your device, we cannot recover it for you. Clearing your browser’s site data, using a private window, or losing the device can mean losing the record. Syncing to your own Drive is the way to keep it across devices.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you use it, your Google account is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy, not by these terms. The Quiz Book uses the sign-in only to label your progress and to keep separate accounts apart on a shared device, and the site never sees your Google password.

If you are a child who is not old enough to hold a Google account, use the site without signing in. Every quiz works that way.
