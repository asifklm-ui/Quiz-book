The Quiz Book

Privacy policy

The Quiz Book is free, and it is built so that there is almost nothing to collect. This page says exactly what happens to your practice, in plain words.

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling of data. Ever.
  • Practice is saved in your own browser, on the device you are using.
  • Syncing is optional and runs through your own Google Drive. There is no server of ours, because there is no server at all — the site is a set of static files.
  • Signing in is optional. Every quiz works without an account.

What is kept in your browser

The Quiz Book keeps its record in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere for this to work. The record is:

  • Your practice: which topics you have answered, how many you got right, and how many questions you have tried in each.
  • Your review pocket and saved quizzes: questions you chose to save for later.
  • Days practised: the dates you practised, used for the streak.
  • Your sign-in record, if you sign in: your Google account name, email address, profile picture link, and an account ID.

If you use a shared device, anyone using that browser profile can see this practice record. Clearing your browser’s site data for quizbookcentral.com deletes it. In private or incognito windows it may be discarded when you close the window.

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The site does not use cookies for tracking at all.

Syncing through your own Google Drive

Sync is off until you turn it on. Signing in asks Google for permission to use one hidden folder in your Google Drive called the app data folder, but that permission is not used until you turn sync on: nothing is read from or written to your Drive before then. Once it is on, the site creates a single file there: quizbook-progress.json.

  • The app data folder is private to this app. The Quiz Book can only see its own file — it cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • The file holds your practice record: topic counts, the questions you saved for review, and the days you practised. It does not contain your name, email address or photo.
  • The file is stored in your Google account, counted against your storage, and is not copied to us.
  • When two devices sync, counts are merged by taking the higher number, so a merge can never remove marks you have earned.

You can stop syncing from the account area of the site at any time; after that, nothing more is uploaded or downloaded. You can also remove the site’s access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Turning sync off or removing access does not delete the practice kept in the browser of that device.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional and is only used to label your progress and to keep separate accounts apart on a device that more than one person uses. Google tells the site your name, your email address, your profile picture link and an account ID. That information stays in your browser, and it is only shared with Google if you turn sync on (Google already knows who you are).

If you never sign in, nothing about you is involved at all — the quizzes work exactly the same.

The two things the home page fetches

To be completely straight with you, the home page makes two requests to other services so that two small decorations work. Neither one is used to identify you, and both fail quietly.

  • The site visit counter. The home page asks a public counter service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) to add one to a single total, once per browser session, so the page can show how many people have visited. It sends no personal information — just the request itself.
  • The weather tile. If you click it and allow your browser’s location prompt, your approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (for the temperature) and BigDataCloud (to turn the coordinates into a place name). Your coordinates are not stored by the site, and the result is kept in memory for ten minutes. If you decline the prompt, the tile simply says “Location off” and everything else keeps working.

Children’s privacy

The Quiz Book is made for kindergarten through grade 12, and it is built to need nothing from a child. It does not ask for a name, an age or a birthday, and it collects nothing on our side because there is no side to collect it.

Signing in is optional and normally happens on a family, school or personal Google account with a grown-up’s help. A child who is not old enough to hold a Google account can use every quiz without signing in; their practice stays on that device only. There are no ads here, so there is nothing to target and no reason to profile anyone.

Where the site is hosted

The Quiz Book is published as static files on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it receives each request and may keep ordinary access logs such as the time, the page requested and the IP address that asked for it. We do not add any analytics or reporting on top of those logs, and we do not use them to follow anyone around the web.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete the practice on a device: clear the site data for quizbookcentral.com in your browser settings. This removes it completely.
  • Stop syncing: use the sync control in the account area, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Drive file: ask using the contact below and the synced file will be removed, or simply keep the site’s access revoked so it is never read again.
  • Nothing to close: there is no account to delete on our side, because we never had one.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it, and anything meaningful — a new kind of data, a new service — will be described here before it happens. It will never quietly become a policy that collects more than this one does.

Questions

Questions about this policy, or a request to remove a synced practice file, can go to the support address shown on The Quiz Book’s Google sign-in screen.
